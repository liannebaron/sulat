Practice

Learn to write a phrase in Baybayin: watch it, trace it, then write it from memory.

    How the lesson works

    1. Watch each syllable being written.
    2. Trace it over the guide.
    3. Write the whole phrase from memory, with no guide. Tap Peek if you get stuck.

    Tip: while you trace, try to remember each shape. You’ll write them on your own at the end.
